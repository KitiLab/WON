# WON Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The WON project, stewarded by KitiLab, is dedicated to providing an inclusive, respectful, and productive environment for all participants, whether contributing through code, documentation, discussions, or issue reports.

## Standards of Conduct

Participants are expected to:
- Communicate with respect, clarity, and constructive intent.
- Focus on technical merit, empirical evidence, and engineering quality.
- Respect differing perspectives and experiences.
- Graciously accept constructive feedback.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, public or private intimidation, and disruptive behavior in project forums.

## Scope and Enforcement

This policy applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue trackers, discussions, and related events. Violations may be reported confidentially to the KitiLab maintainers team. Maintainers retain the right to remove offensive comments, close disruptive issues, and suspend access for repeat violations.
